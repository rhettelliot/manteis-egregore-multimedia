# Egregore Multimedia — Master Style Guide

> **Project Context:** This style guide governs all Egregore multimedia — guided audio meditations, educational YouTube videos, and visual content for the 16 Egregore instruments and protocols. Every asset must feel like it was produced by a single intelligence operating with one aesthetic vision.

## 1. The Core Philosophy: "Bio-Tactical, Not Boutique"

The Egregore is NOT a meditation app. It is NOT Headspace. It is NOT Calm. It is a system of cognitive sovereignty tools disguised as psychological operations field manuals. The aesthetic is **Swiss science poster meets avant-garde industrial design meets classified document declassification.**

* **The Logic:** Every visual is a diagram, a schematic, or a data readout — not a photograph of someone sitting cross-legged. The Egregore doesn't show you peace. It shows you the *architecture* of peace.
* **The Tone:** Military brevity meets philosophical depth. Like an aircraft safety card for the mind. Clear, direct, no-nonsense, but carrying genuine weight.
* **The Juxtaposition:** Clinical terminology + deep human meaning. "Neurocomputational Harm" is a technical term for something that is happening to you right now. The visual language makes the abstract visceral.

## 2. The Visual Language

### Colors (FROZEN — match the sites)
| Token | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Canvas | `#0D0F12` | Background. Always. Not pure black. Not navy. |
| Surface | `#15181E` | Solid panels, cards, text backgrounds |
| Signal | `#FF5500` | Solar orange. RARE. One accent per frame. Never as fill. |
| Ink | `#F4F3EE` | Primary text. Cream, not white. |
| Ink Ghost | `#F4F3EE33` | Large faint background numbers, grid lines |
| Mono Data | `#9EA4B0` | Technical labels, coordinates, metrics |
| Positive | `#00D455` | Status: active, healthy, deployed |
| Alert | `#007AFF` | Status: signal, attention, monitoring |
| Critical | `#FF3B30` | Status: threat, danger, breach |

### Typography
* **Inter** — ALL display, body, UI, labels, voiceover text overlays. No exceptions.
* **JetBrains Mono** — DATA ONLY. Coordinates, timestamps, frequency readouts, breath counts, biometric metrics. Never for prose.
* **NO SERIF.** Ever. Not for quotes, not for chapter titles, not for "wisdom."
* **NO GRADIENT TEXT.** Ever.

### Geometry
* **Border radius: 0px** on all structural panels, buttons, cards. Sharp corners ONLY.
* **12-20px** allowed ONLY on atmospheric background elements (glows, orbs) — never on UI.
* **No circles on cover art.** No rounded avatars.
* **Grid lines as structure.** Thin white lines at 8% opacity, visible as architectural elements.
* **Large faint background numbers** (01, 02, 03) in cream at 15-20% opacity — chapter/scene markers.

### BANNED (Immediate rejection)
* Glassmorphism, blur, translucency
* Planetary halos, neon edge glows, particle systems
* Custom cursors, noise grain (unless explicitly requested for a specific scene)
* Gradient text, decorative section numbers, tracked uppercase eyebrows
* Monospace used as costume (mono only for actual data)
* Identical fade-up stagger on every section
* Photographs of people meditating, candles, incense, mountains at sunset, lotus flowers
* Soft pastel colors, rounded corners, gentle gradients
* Any imagery that could be mistaken for a mainstream meditation/wellness app

## 3. The Imagery — Abstract Technical Schematics

All AI-generated stills follow the Manteis Design Standard: **abstract, technical, geometric.**

### Approved Visual Categories
1. **Schematic diagrams** — breath patterns as waveforms, neural pathways as circuit traces, energy channels as PCB layouts
2. **HUD elements** — viewfinder brackets, targeting reticles, coordinate markers, radar-scope graphics
3. **Data visualization** — frequency graphs, biometric readouts, system architecture maps
4. **Swiss grid compositions** — numbered panels, monospace labels, thin connecting lines, orange signal nodes
5. **Abstract textures** — dark slate surfaces, metallic finishes, crystalline structures (obsidian, quartz), liquid metal, cymatic patterns
6. **Cross-section diagrams** — anatomical cross-sections rendered as technical schematics (not medical illustrations)
7. **Classified document aesthetics** — redacted text, declassification stamps, file numbers, security clearances

### AI Prompt Template (Golden Rule)
Every still prompt must follow this structure:

> **[SUBJECT]** rendered as a **[DIAGRAM TYPE: schematic/HUD/cross-section/data viz/Swiss grid]** on dark slate #0D0F12. **[GEOMETRIC ELEMENTS: rectangles, nodes, lines, dots, waveforms]** in **[COLORS: orange #FF5500, cream #F4F3EE, blue-grey #9EA4B0]**. Thin white grid lines at 8% opacity. A large faint number **[XX]** in cream at 15% opacity. Monospace-style labels in muted blue-grey #9EA4B0. Swiss design brutalism. Sharp corners. 8K resolution. No photographs. No people. No text. No organic shapes unless diegetic to the concept.

## 4. The Motion — Remotion Overlay Specs

Remotion layers the Swiss design system ON TOP of the AI-generated stills and animations. The stills provide the visual container; Remotion adds the information layer.

### Approved Remotion Components
1. **KineticTypography** — Voiceover text types out word by word with blinking cursor. Used for key phrases, chapter titles, protocol names. Inter font, cream #F4F3EE, cursor in orange #FF5500.
2. **AnimatedCounter** — Breath counts (4 in, 4 hold, 4 out), frequency readouts (16 BPM → 8 BPM → 4 BPM), timer displays. JetBrains Mono, orange #FF5500.
3. **TerminalAnimation** — Protocol instructions displayed as terminal commands. Red/yellow/green dots. Used for "system initialization" sequences.
4. **StatReveal** — Key stats slam in with spring. "72,000 CIRCUITS" or "20 SECONDS" or "ONE CYCLE PER MINUTE." Large, bold, orange on dark.
5. **BackgroundImage** — AI-generated stills with Ken Burns parallax (slow zoom + pan). Dark gradient overlay at 40-60% opacity for text readability.
6. **PhaseCard** — Chapter/phase cards spring in. "CHAPTER 01: THE URBAN WARZONE" with status badge. Sharp corners, solid surface, orange accent line.
7. **Custom: BreathVisualizer** — Animated breath circle that expands/contracts with breath counts. Not a generic meditation bubble — a technical ring with monospace count overlay. Orange ring on dark. Sharp. Mechanical. NOT soft.
8. **Custom: FrequencyGraph** — Animated waveform that shifts as the narration describes different breath rates. 16 BPM = fast jagged waves. 8 BPM = slower. 4 BPM = long smooth waves. 1 cycle/min = single massive wave. Monospace frequency labels.

### Motion Rules
* **One signature moment per video.** Motion communicates state changes, not decoration.
* **Content visible by default.** Never hide with `initial: { opacity: 0 }`.
* **Pacing follows the voice.** Text reveals sync to voiceover rhythm. Slow narration = slow reveals. Rapid briefing = fast cuts.
* **No ambient floating particles.** No decorative animation. Every movement means something.

* **Secondary (Educational Videos):** edge-tts `en-US-ChristopherMultilingualNeural` at normal rate — documentary authority. Clinical. Clear. Like a briefing officer.

* **Rhett's own voice:** Always an option. He may record certain pieces himself.

### Music Bed
* **Source:** Manteis Recordings catalog — 9 releases of deep textural electronic music.
* **Usage:** Ambient underscore at 10-20% volume during narration. Swells to 30-40% during silent practice periods. Never competes with voice.
* **Selection:** Match the emotional register of the piece. BREATHER = tense, tactical, building. FILTER = contemplative, structured. FOCUS = steady, rhythmic. SUBSTRATE = vast, cosmic, deep.

### Sound Design
* **Breath cues:** Subtle tone or chime at inhale/hold/exhale transitions. NOT a bell — a low frequency pulse. Like a sonar ping or a medical monitor tone.
* **Ambient bed:** Dark drone, low frequency. Not nature sounds. Not singing bowls. Electronic. Textural. The sound of a system running.
* **Transitions:** Hard cuts between sections. No gentle crossfades. This is a field briefing, not a spa.
