## BREATHER-Specific Style Notes

**The BREATHER aesthetic extends the master style with:**
* **Tactical language:** "threat architecture," "neural aegis," "hard target," "signature management." The visuals match — this looks like a classified briefing, not a wellness video.
* **The mechanical ring:** The breath visualizer is NOT a soft expanding bubble. It's a technical instrument — a sharp, thin, orange ring that expands and contracts with mechanical precision. Think sonar display, not yoga studio.
* **The frequency graph:** The waveform comparison (16 BPM jagged vs 4 BPM smooth) is the central visual metaphor. It appears in the opening and returns throughout the meditation as a tracking display.
* **Color coding:** Orange = the operator's signal (sovereign). The chaos above the border line uses a desaturated red-orange to signal threat. The clean signals below use pure orange #FF5500. The distinction is subtle but intentional.
* **No human bodies:** The breath is visualized through the mechanical ring and the frequency graph, never through a person's chest rising and falling. The body is implied by the data.
